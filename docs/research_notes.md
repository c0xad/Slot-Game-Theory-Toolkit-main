# Research Notes

This section contains miscellaneous notes, literature review summaries, and potential future research directions.

## Literature Review Snippets

*   **Markov Models:** Useful for state transitions in bonuses, but complexity grows quickly. (Ref: MDPI source)
*   **Volatility & Time-on-Device:** Lower Coefficient of Variation (CV) correlates with longer play sessions. (Ref: ResearchGate source)
*   **Live-RTP Monitoring:** UKGC mandates statistical checks; deviations require explanation. (Ref: Gambling Commission)
*   **Reel Optimization:** Search heuristics like VNS can find good-enough reel strips for target RTPs. (Ref: ResearchGate source)
